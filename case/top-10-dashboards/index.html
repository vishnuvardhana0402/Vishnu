Three people, one order.

The store team packs it. A rider delivers it. Head office keeps an eye on every store. Each of them needs to know where the order is, and what to do next.

Accepts it within minutes, checks stock, records each phone’s IMEI and packs it.

Collects it at the counter with an OTP and takes it to the customer. Or the customer collects it themselves.

STORE ADMIN · ORDER #1001

A new order pops up on the store’s screen.

THE PROBLEM Staff are busy serving customers in the shop, so an online order can easily be missed.

WHAT WE DESIGNED Every new order pops up over the screen, with a timer and the stock left for each item.

One tap at a time, from shop to customer.

THE PROBLEM Many people handle an order, and a step done in the wrong order causes mistakes, like packing a phone before noting its IMEI.

WHAT WE DESIGNED Each step has a colour and one button for what comes next, not a dropdown to pick from. Plus proof at every hand-over.

    • STORE ADMIN · WHEN SOMETHING GOES WRONG

    When a delivery fails, the order says why.

    THE PROBLEM Sometimes the customer isn’t home, or the phone arrives damaged. The store needs to know what happened and what to do next.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED The order changes to Reschedule or Replace, with the reason written on it and one button for the next step.

    The customer wasn’t home.

    The rider brings the order back. Staff mark it At store and give it to a new rider. The reason stays on the order.

    The phone arrived damaged.

    The customer’s photos go on the order. Staff pack a new phone, note its IMEI and send it with a rider.

    STORE ADMIN · DELIVERY PARTNER

    Every rider is checked before their first order.

    THE PROBLEM Staff hand phones worth over a lakh to riders. They need to know each rider is who they say they are.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED A new rider sends their Aadhaar, PAN and driving licence. Staff check each one and approve. Then the rider’s orders show on their profile.

    THE PROBLEM Besides orders, the store team needs to know what’s in stock, whether the store is taking orders, and how fast orders leave.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED Each one is a simple screen in the same dashboard, a tap away from the orders.

    Super Admin

    The dashboard head office uses to see every store’s orders, add products, run offers and set the rules.

    SUPER ADMIN · ORDERS

    Every store’s orders, in one list.

    THE PROBLEM Orders come in for every store at once. Head office needs to see all of them, and where each one is, without calling each store.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED One list of every order, with its store and its status colour. Filter by store or status, and open any order to see what the store sees.

    This screen works. Open any item in the menu, or order #1001.

    SUPER ADMIN · PRODUCTS

    Add a product once, for every store.

    THE PROBLEM Top-10 sells phones and accessories from many brands. Each one needs its details, price, colours and the rules for how it’s delivered.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED One Add Product form. Pick a category and the right fields follow. Price, EMI, colours and delivery rules sit in the same place.

    Open box delivery The box is opened and filmed at the door

    IMEI Number Verification The phone’s number is noted before packing

  • Name it, pick a category The category decides which details the form asks for.
  • Add the price and colours Regular and sale price, EMI, and the colours it comes in.
  • Switch on the delivery rules Open-box delivery and IMEI checks, for this product.
  • Set it Active It goes live on the app and website.

    • SUPER ADMIN · ANALYTICS

    See how each store is doing, side by side.

    THE PROBLEM Head office needs to know which stores are doing well and which need help, and where shoppers give up before they pay.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED A view that puts any two stores side by side, and a funnel that shows each step from the homepage to a placed order.

    SUPER ADMIN · THE CUSTOMER APP

    The app, offers and messages, run from one place.

    THE PROBLEM Sales and new phones change every week. The app’s homepage, its coupons and its messages have to keep up.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED Head office reorders the homepage by dragging, makes a coupon in one of three types, and messages customers or store owners, now or later.

    What’s on the homepage?

    Content Management: drag a section and the app follows

    What’s on offer?

    20% off the order No minimum purchase amount

    20% off a product Applies to Smartphone

    Buy 2, get 1 free Specific category: Mobile & Accessories

    Coupons: three types, each with a start date Pick a type

    Who needs to know?

    Notifications: to customers or store owners, now or later

    SUPER ADMIN · STORES AND SETTINGS

    New stores, staff and rules, set up from head office.

    THE PROBLEM As Top-10 grows, head office adds stores and staff, decides who can do what, and keeps the rules the same everywhere.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED Add a store with its address, hours and contact. Add staff and set their store permissions. Write each policy once.

    One set of parts for both dashboards.

    WHAT WE DESIGNED One small system both dashboards share: a colour and a next step for each order status, one type family, one icon set and the same components.

    Every status has a colour, and one button for what’s next.
