CASE STUDY 04 TOP-10 MOBILES RESPONSIVE WEBSITE

The whole store, in any browser.

No app to install. Top-10 Mobiles on a phone, an iPad or a desktop, in the browser people already use.

Photo: Robert Katzki, Unsplash

Easy access, on any screen.

Not everyone wants to download an app. The website gives them the same store in the browser: shop, choose delivery or store pickup, and follow the order, on a phone, an iPad or a desktop.

One store, laid out for each screen.

The same parts, arranged again for each width. Pick a size, or let it run.

Desktop The full search bar and your address in the header, and rows of products side by side.

Tablet The same header, fewer items to a row, and the rest of each row a swipe away.

Phone The header shrinks to the logo and your address, with a strip above it that offers the app.

Shopping, in four easy steps.

Each step on a desktop and on a phone.

Home Offers, categories and brands, with the delivery address up top.

Find it Search, or open a category, then filter by brand, price or RAM.

Delivery or pickup When it can arrive, or which stores nearby have it today.

Check out One page, with the total always in view, then a one-time code to sign in.

Then follow the order.

Each status, the time it’s due and who’s bringing it. On a desktop, a QR code opens the app for the live map.

Confirmed When it should arrive, and the store packing it.

On the way The time left, and the delivery code to share at the door.

Delivered When it arrived, with the bill close by.

Your orders Deliveries and pickups in one place, each with its status.

The whole store, no app needed.

The same easy steps on a phone, an iPad or a desktop, with delivery or pickup from a store nearby.

People who won’t install an app can still buy, and follow their order.

One set of parts in Figma, laid out again for each width.
